FACTS:   Arrested 23 April 1849, eight days after reading Belinsky's letter to Gogol to the Petrashevsky circle.
         Eight months in the Peter and Paul Fortress. Mock execution on Semyonovsky Square, 22 December 1849:
         white shirts, the first three tied to posts, Dostoevsky in the second three ("no more than a minute left to live",
         his letter to his brother that day). Drums, then an aide-de-camp with Nicholas I's commutation, arranged beforehand.
         Nikolai Grigoryev, one of the first three, lost his mind. Four years of hard labour at Omsk in irons.
         He was 27 when arrested and 28 on the square (born 11 November 1821).
