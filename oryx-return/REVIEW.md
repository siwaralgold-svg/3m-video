# Review · المها أبو حراب

## Creative calls
- Built straight from the T3M storyboard kit (8 shots + the added closing line «الأمل ما يختفي… إذا لقى أحد يحميه»). No alternative storylines: the kit is the brief.
- The kit's art plates were split into a landscape plate + one cut-out per silhouette, so animals, people and the hand move on their own (the kit asks for separate layers, not slides).
- Shots 7 and 8 were re-cut on the voice: "ما زال يحتاج حماية" stays on the status-card scene, and the cut to the adult and calf lands on «لكن رجع».
- Animals that sit over patched plate areas stay put (partnership oryx, left release oryx); only animals over flat ground walk, so no seams show.

## Measured
- `check`: passes. Rendered file: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 40.0 s, −16.7 LUFS integrated, −1.6 dBFS peak.
- Beat, seam and phone sheets looked at; text inside the 168–1330 band.
- The sound was measured, not heard: effect timings come from the manifest's `peak_at`.

## Kept on purpose
- `line_unread` on «إذا لقى أحد يحميه» (≈1.7 s on screen): the voice says it at 38.3 s and the kit caps the film at 40 s. Allowing 40.5 s would clear it.
- No music bed: no music source is connected in this session.

## Weakest shot / next
- s2 (Abu Dhabi → Chad) is 1.95 s and very plain; a light ground texture or a small oryx moving along the line would help.
- Possible additions: a soft music bed, a T3M logo end card.
