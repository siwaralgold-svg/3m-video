# Review · المها أبو حراب

## Creative calls
- Built straight from the T3M storyboard kit (8 shots + the added closing line «الأمل ما يختفي… إذا لقى أحد يحميه»). No alternative storylines: the kit is the brief.
- The kit's art plates were split into a landscape plate + one cut-out per silhouette, so animals, people and the hand move on their own (the kit asks for separate layers, not slides).
- Shots 7 and 8 were re-cut on the voice: "ما زال يحتاج حماية" stays on the status-card scene, and the cut to the adult and calf lands on «لكن رجع».
- Animals that sit over patched plate areas stay put (partnership oryx, left release oryx); only animals over flat ground walk, so no seams show.

## Revision 2 (requested)
- Hand removed. Opening is now a running herd with a generated gallop track (footstep samples from sfx-library arranged per animal); the herd and its sound thin out one by one from «اختفى». Film is 44 s.
- The running pose is the kit's oryx outline cut into body + four legs, swung per a gallop cycle; a stylised gallop, not a real gait.

## Revision 3 (requested, from the person's references)
- Opening rebuilt from their run-cycle sheet: six gallop poses (assets/art/gallop_*.png) cycled per animal with phase offsets, on three depth rows (12 back, 9 middle, 2 front).
- 2.7–4.8 s the herd eases to a walk (jointed walk rig); 4.8–5.9 s back, middle and front fade in turn; one oryx in front walks alone and fades by 6.2 s; empty land until the cut.
- Hoof track follows the same clock: gallop taks while running, soft grass steps while walking, each animal's steps stop when it fades.

## Measured
- `check`: passes. Rendered file: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 44.0 s, −16.8 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBFS peak; gallop intro −20.9 LUFS.
- Beat, seam and phone sheets looked at; text inside the 168–1330 band.
- The sound was measured, not heard: effect timings come from the manifest's `peak_at`.

## Kept on purpose
- `line_unread` on «ما زال يحتاج حماية» (≈1.5 s): it is spoken, and the cut on «لكن رجع» follows it.
- No music bed: no music source is connected in this session.

## Weakest shot / next
- s2 (Abu Dhabi → Chad) is 1.95 s and very plain; a light ground texture or a small oryx moving along the line would help.
- Possible additions: a soft music bed, a T3M logo end card.
