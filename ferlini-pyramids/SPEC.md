# فيرليني: ملفّين — spec

GOAL:    the viewer learns who took the tops off Sudan's pyramids, hears the fair side, and gives a verdict.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1080×1920, 30 fps, TikTok.
TIME:    89.2 s. Clock: the ElevenLabs voiceover (assets/voiceover.mp3, 83.6 s); word times in assets/transcript.json.
         Scenes read `w(<word index>)` from assets/times.js, which `python3 tools/build.py` writes together with
         index.html, the captions and the sound-effect starts.
LOOK:    the Edison cartoon style (../edison-stole/STYLE.md). Places: Meroe at night (navy sky, brown dunes,
         sandstone pyramids #d9a868), an open-air court with a stone table, a museum hall, the sea.
         Gold #CEA532 once a scene at most: جوزيبي فيرليني, عينه على الذهب, حجر حجر, ذهب, سبقوه بقرون, أكيد مزوّر,
         في ألمانيا, للأبد, الحكم لك أنت. The treasure itself is gold wherever it appears.
OBJECTS: Ferlini (red tarboosh, moustache, chin beard), Queen Amanishakheto (skullcap crown, gold collar),
         a museum curator, the king of Bavaria, a hooded tomb robber. Pyramids are built of courses (`EK.pyr`)
         so they can be cut, taken apart and rebuilt.
