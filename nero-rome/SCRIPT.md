## To check before publishing

- «ستة أيام»: the length of the fire in Tacitus, after which it flared up again.
- «خارج روما»: Tacitus places Nero at Antium when the fire began.
- «شوارع أوسع»: Tacitus also lists limits on building height and building in stone.
- «دوّر على متّهم غيره»: Tacitus' own explanation for the charge against the Christians; he is the only main source for it.
